## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Sizing Metric

**Reason**: Story points are removed. The team works in continuous flow, with no velocity and no schedule based on estimates. Points were only used to decide when to split a story, and that is a judgement call that needs no scale.
**Migration**: `docs/sizing.md` is deleted. Splitting is covered by **Oversized Stories Are Split** in `documentation/epics`.

### Requirement: Sizing Scale

**Reason**: Story points are removed.
**Migration**: None.

### Requirement: Published Baseline Anchor

**Reason**: Story points are removed.
**Migration**: None. `01-S03` stays first in the backlog because it builds the walking skeleton, not because it was the anchor.

### Requirement: Estimation Dimensions

**Reason**: Story points are removed.
**Migration**: Unknowns are still handled by unverified assumptions and spikes (`process/backlog-flow`).

### Requirement: Rationale Is Mandatory

**Reason**: Story points are removed.
**Migration**: Each epic's REQUIREMENTS SIZING section is deleted.

### Requirement: Splitting Threshold

**Reason**: The limit was measured in story points.
**Migration**: See **Oversized Stories Are Split** in `documentation/epics`. A story that can't be delivered by one change is split.

### Requirement: Stated Estimate Uncertainty

**Reason**: Story points are removed.
**Migration**: None.

### Requirement: Cross-Document Consistency

**Reason**: The roll-up table it kept consistent is removed.
**Migration**: None.

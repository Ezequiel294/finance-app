## Evolution & Why This Version Changed

### 1. What was deficient in this initial version?
* **Generic archetypes lacking authentic local context:** The initial personas featured a generic US college graduate (*Devon Miller*) living in Denver with standard US student loans and checking accounts.
* **Failure to represent multi-currency and account-isolation workflows:** Did not capture the critical security practice of segregating savings in Banco Nacional "sobres" or daily top-up routines between USD salary accounts and CRC debit cards.
* **Disconnected from the primary user's lived reality:** The app's actual builder and primary user had specific student and cross-border investing needs that were unrepresented.

### 2. What new constraints or insights prompted the change?
* The project author provided direct personal context: fourth-year software engineering student living independently away from parents, managing rent/groceries in Colones, receiving salary in Dollars, and investing in VOO/BTC using `Inversiones.xlsx`.

### 3. Decisions incorporated into Canonical v2 (`docs/02-personas.md`):
* Substituted *Devon Miller* with **Ezequiel Martinez — The Independent Student & Cross-Border Builder** as the primary anchor persona.
* Retained *Priya Sharma* (Corporate Multi-Asset Investor) and *Marcus Vance* (SRE / Automation Power User) to preserve diverse user coverage across different financial tiers.
